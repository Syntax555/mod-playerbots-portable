Selected historical quest, trainer, spell, rank, triggered-effect and quest
experience data are adapted to the server's per-character progression gates,
generated era Spell.dbc records and Individual Progression's custom_quest_xp_tbc
and custom_quest_xp_level tables. No complete CMaNGOS database or core is distributed here. The source
revisions and local adaptations are recorded in versions.lock.json and in the
client package's SOURCE_MANIFEST.json.

Authorship remains with the CMaNGOS Project and the contributors identified in
each pinned repository's AUTHORS file and commit history. Classic-DB also
credits the Zero Project team and the MaNGOS Foundation team.
